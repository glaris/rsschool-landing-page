Artist Statement

Each ink drawing captures a singular moment leaving no room for error. Every line on paper becomes a definitive manifesto of graphic space.

About the Artist

Evgeny Kreshchensky is a professional fine artist and scenographer based in Belgrade. His distinct graphic style is defined by a painstaking technique of thousands of miniature strokes, lines, and dots. Working with exceptional precision in pen and ink, Evgeny uses this intricate method to capture human forms, deep emotions, and subtle moods on paper.
