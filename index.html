Statement

Each ink drawing captures a singular moment where there is no room for error. A line left on paper becomes a definitive manifesto of graphic space.

About the Artist

Evgeny Kreshchensky is a contemporary graphic artist and scenographer. His work explores the boundaries of academic drawing, merging strict theatrical geometry with the vivid expression of ink and pen.
